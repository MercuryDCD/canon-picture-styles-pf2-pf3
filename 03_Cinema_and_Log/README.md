# Cinema & Log Picture Profiles

> Log curves, ultra-flat profiles, and movie-grade color gradations engineered for maximum dynamic range retention, video shooting, and color grading workflows.

**Total profiles in this category:** `41`

## Overview & Key Highlights

* **Technicolor CineStyle**: The industry standard flat Log curve for Canon DSLR/mirrorless cameras, maximizing latitude for post-production grading.
* **Flaat Series (Flaat 1 to Flaat 4)**: Similaar's acclaimed logarithmic tone curves engineered to extend dynamic range without introducing extreme noise.
* **Marvels Cine (1.2, 2.1 Panalog, 3.4)**: Classic indie film profile with clean shadows, organic midtone contrast, and pleasing filmic roll-off.
* **California Cowboy & EuroTrip**: Narrative travel grades offering warm golden cinematic washes or melancholic European cool tones in-camera.
* **Panavision Genesis & 300 Bleach Bypass**: High-end digital cinema emulations and harsh, desaturated silver retention looks.
