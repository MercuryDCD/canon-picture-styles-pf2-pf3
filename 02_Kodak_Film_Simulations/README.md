# Kodak Film Simulations (Portra, Kodachrome, Ektachrome)

> Emulations of iconic Kodak analog stocks characterized by warm golden hour undertones, rich color palettes, and natural dynamic range.

**Total profiles in this category:** `9`

## Overview & Key Highlights

* **Kodak Portra 400 (Standard & Extended DR)**: The quintessential portrait film. Warm skin tones, lifted shadows, and gentle highlight retention designed for natural light.
* **Kodak Portra 160NC & Soft**: Natural Color (NC) variant optimized for minimal saturation, delicate skin textures, and high-key fashion.
* **Kodachrome 25 & 64 (RetroKrum)**: Legendary National Geographic slide film with distinctive deep reds, rich blacks, and nostalgic vintage color harmony.
* **Kodak Ektachrome (EES & Cross Process)**: Vibrant color transparency film alongside creative cross-processing (E-6 developed in C-41 chemistry).
