# Creative, Japanese Aesthetic, Lomo & Special Looks

> Artistic creator profiles, anime/pastel tones, toy camera effects, infrared emulations, and seasonal palettes.

**Total profiles in this category:** `99`

## Overview & Key Highlights

* **Kevin Wang Japanese Style**: The iconic bright, ethereal pastel palette with lifted airy shadows popular in Japanese portraiture and street photography.
* **Kevin Wang LOMO & RikoLomo**: High saturation, vignetted toy-camera look reminiscent of analog cross-processed Lomography.
* **Infrared Simulation (IR / HalfIR / FakeIR)**: Simulates infrared film by turning foliage white and rendering clear skies near-black.
* **Canon Official Specials (Nostalgia, Twilight, Emerald, Autumn Hues)**: Canon's discontinued creative Picture Style series for 70s print looks, golden autumn leaves, and twilight skies.
* **Japanese Seasonal (Wagokoro, Akiiro, Tsuyazakura)**: Cultural aesthetics for cherry blossoms, autumn foliage, and traditional zen architecture.
