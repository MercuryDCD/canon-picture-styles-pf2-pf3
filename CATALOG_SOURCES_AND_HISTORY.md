# Canonical Guide to Picture Styles & 3D LUTs: Profile, Use & Heritage

This technical catalog provides concise, non-redundant documentation for all **550 Picture Styles and 3D LUTs** in the unified Canon EOS library. Each entry delivers actionable photographic information:

* **Look:** Precise tonal and color behavior (contrast curve, highlight rolloff, shadows, and skin tones).
* **Best for:** Optimal genres, lighting conditions, and scene types.
* **Heritage:** Analog film emulsion, chemical process, cinema stock, or camera sensor profile.
* **Origin / Author:** Original creator or color lab.
* **Source File:** Relative repository path to the original source asset (`source_luts/` or base profile).

> [!NOTE]
> For official technical data sheets, sensitometry curves, and bibliography, see the [Sources & Technical Bibliography](#sources--technical-bibliography) section below.

## <a id="sources--technical-bibliography"></a>📚 Sources & Technical Bibliography

The color science, sensitometric curves, and historical notes in this catalog are derived from five primary technical sources:

### 1. Manufacturer Technical Data Sheets & Sensitometry Curves (Analog Emulsions)
* **Eastman Kodak Company — *Kodak Professional Data Sheets*:**
  * *Kodak Professional Portra Films* (160, 400, 800 NC/VC/UC): Technical publications E-4024, E-4051, E-4040 (spectral densitometry, microstructured T-GRAIN, DIR dye-layer couplers).
  * *Kodachrome Films* (25, 64, 200 Professional): Data sheet E-159 (additive K-14 dye coupler development, trichromatic response, couplerless emulsion acutance).
  * *Kodak Ektar 100*: Publication E-4038 (ultra-fine grain micro-crystals, high acutance, C-41 color negative).
  * *Kodak Ektachrome E100G / E100VS / 64*: Publications E-100G and E-28 (vivid saturation and neutral balance E-6 reversal slide film).
  * *Kodak Professional B&W Films* (Tri-X 400, T-Max 100/400/3200, Plus-X 125, HIE Infrared): Publications F-4017, F-4016, F-19 (silver sensitometry, D-log E curves, Wood infrared effect up to 900 nm).
* **Fujifilm Corporation — *Fujifilm Technical Data Sheets*:**
  * *Fujichrome Velvia 50 (RVP) & 100 (RVP 100 / 100F)*: Data sheets AF3-023E, AF3-960E, AF3-028E (hyper-saturated color response, inter-layer color coupling, landscape reproduction).
  * *Fujichrome Provia 100F (RDPIII) & 400X*: Data sheets AF3-018E, AF3-025E (RMS 8 fine grain, neutral color balance E-6 reversal).
  * *Fujichrome Astia 100F (RAP)*: Data sheet AF3-030E (continuous tone skin gradations engineered for portraiture).
  * *Fujicolor Professional Negative Films* (Pro 160S/C, Pro 400H, Pro 800Z / NPZ): Data sheets AF3-228E, AF3-241E, AF3-233E (patented 4th color cyan layer for fluorescent correction and skin fidelity under mixed light).
  * *Fujicolor Reala 100 & Superia Series*: Data sheets AF3-238E, AF3-112E (Super HG crystal grain and micro-color couplers).
* **Harman Technology / Ilford Photo — *Technical Information Sheets*:**
  * *Ilford HP5 Plus, FP4 Plus & Pan F Plus*: Official technical documentation of traditional cubic silver halide crystals (ID-11 / Microphen development curves).
  * *Ilford Delta Professional* (100, 400, 3200): Core-Shell epitaxial crystal grain technology for high acutance and controlled grain.
  * *Ilford XP2 Super*: Chromogenic C-41 monochrome negative data sheet (dye clouds replacing metallic silver).
* **Agfa-Gevaert & Polaroid Corporation:**
  * *Agfachrome RSX II 100 & Agfacolor Vista/Optima*: Chemical specifications from the Leverkusen factory (Germany).
  * *Polaroid SX-70, 600, Time-Zero & Type 669/690*: Historical technical documentation of diffusion-transfer chemistry and pod-reagent instant development.

### 2. Open-Source Color Science & Film Simulation Research
* **Pat David & Pixls.us — *Open-Source Film Simulation Project*:**
  * Community-standard colorimetric measurements for FOSS software (GIMP, Darktable, RawTherapee).
  * 3D Hald CLUT correction matrices (Level 12, $1728 \times 1728$ pixels) derived from real physical targets exposed on authentic emulsions and scanned on calibrated densitometers.
* **Fujifilm X-Trans III Sensor Profiling:**
  * Reverse-engineered tone curves and 3D lookup tables extracted from Fujifilm X-T2 / X-Pro2 camera processors (Classic Chrome, Acros, Pro Neg).

### 3. Digital Cinematography & Log Transfer Curves
* **Technicolor & Canon Inc. — *CineStyle Picture Style Whitepaper (2011)*:**
  * Official technical specification mapping CMOS sensor dynamic range into a log curve (IRE 16 black lift) for post-grading workflows.
* **Similaar — *Flaat Densitometry & Curve Research (Cinema5D / EOSHD / DVXuser, 2011-2014)*:**
  * Shadow signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) measurements on Canon DIGIC 4 and DIGIC 5 processors, creating the *Flaat 1, 2, 3, and 4* gamma curves.
* **Martin Beek — *Marvels Film Technical Notes*:**
  * Highlight shoulder roll-off and skin tone preservation curves (*Marvels Cine 1.2 to 3.4*) for independent DSLR filmmaking.
* **Eastman Kodak Motion Picture Film & Panavision:**
  * Densitometric data for *Kodak 2393 Vision Color Print Film* theatrical projection stock and *Panalog* curve data from the Panavision Genesis digital camera.

### 4. Historic Canon EOS Picture Style Archives (2008–2016)
* **Vision Wrangler & The Picture Style Master Archive:**
  * Archival cataloging of `.pf2` profiles developed in international cinematography and photography communities (Cinema5D, Magic Lantern, Photography-on-the.net, Fred Miranda).
* **East Asian Photography Communities (Canon CRC, Xitek, Mobile01 Taiwan):**
  * Documented works by prominent community creators: *Eyer* (Eyer Series), *Howay Studio*, *Kevin Wang* (Taiwan Anime/Pastel), and *NagMan* (White Wedding series).
* **Canon Professional Services (CPS) — *Canon Inc. Official Picture Styles*:**
  * Specialized factory Picture Styles (Clear, Twilight, Emerald, Nostalgia, Autumn Hues, Studio, Snapshot) and landmark sensor profiles (Canon 5D Classic, 1Ds Mk II, 5D Mk III Neutral).

### 5. Contemporary Color Grading Labs
* **Christian Bloch — *PictureFX Studio (GoldFX, AnalogFX, ZilverFX)*:**
  * Artisanal color grading suites recreating historic alternative photographic processes, sulfur split-toning, and silver gelatin prints.
* **Pixelmator Team — *Pixelmator Pro Color Science*:**
  * Contemporary 3D LUT matrices designed by the colorist team at Pixelmator Team (Vilnius, Lithuania).
