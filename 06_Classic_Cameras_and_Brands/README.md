# Classic Camera Color Renditions & Brands

> Cross-brand color emulations and classic sensor color science from vintage DSLRs and film manufacturers.

**Total profiles in this category:** `11`

## Overview & Key Highlights

* **Nikon D2X (Mode 1 & Whibal)**: Recreates the praised portrait color rendition of Nikon's flagship D2X professional DSLR.
* **Olympus Blue (Deep Sky)**: Emulates Olympus' famous saturated, deep turquoise-blue sky science.
* **Agfa RSX II 100**: European color reversal slide film with distinctive earthy warm tones.
* **Canon 5D Classic & 1Ds Mark II**: Brings back the organic, film-like color rendering of Canon's original full-frame CCD/CMOS sensors.
