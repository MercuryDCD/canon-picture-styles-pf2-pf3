# Analog Black & White and Monochrome

> Classic monochromatic film stocks and fine-art curves featuring rich tonal gradients, micro-contrast, and authentic darkroom textures.

**Total profiles in this category:** `14`

## Overview & Key Highlights

* **Kodak T-Max 400**: Tabular-grain (T-Grain) film emulation with smooth gray gradations, sharp micro-contrast, and modern rendering.
* **Kodak Tri-X (T-Rex Series)**: Photojournalism legend with bold, punchy blacks, heavy grit, and high-contrast street aesthetic.
* **Cinema Filmic B&W**: Log-derived black and white curve that avoids crushing shadows or blowing highlights.
* **Monochrome Gradation Steps (1 to 5)**: Systematic progression from soft, low-contrast grayscale (1) to deep, dramatic high-contrast punch (5).
