# Fujifilm Film Simulations (Color & Monochrome)

> Emulations of legendary Fujifilm analog film stocks tailored for Canon sensors. Known for organic color rendition, beautiful portrait skin tones, and distinct slide/negative film aesthetics.

**Total profiles in this category:** `28`

## Overview & Key Highlights

* **Astia (100F / Pop / Retro / Vintage)**: Renowned for delicate, smooth portrait skin tones and soft highlight roll-off with gentle contrast.
* **Provia (100F / RDP III)**: Fujifilm's reference color reversal film: realistic, neutral, and faithful colors for versatile all-around photography.
* **Velvia (50 / 100F / RVP)**: High contrast, ultra-saturated slide film famous for striking greens, deep blues, and dramatic landscape photography.
* **Pro Neg (Hi / Std)**: Professional studio negative films: 'Hi' gives sharp contrast on clothing/backgrounds while protecting skin; 'Std' gives soft gradations.
* **Pro 400H / Pro 160S / 160C**: Beloved wedding and editorial negative films featuring muted pastels, signature mint greens, and luminous skin.
* **Superia Reala / FP-100C / Frontier**: Classic 35mm consumer negatives, Fuji instant pack film, and authentic Fuji Frontier minilab digital rendering.
