# Studio Portrait & Skin Tone Optimization

> Calibrated matrices and tonal curves created to flatter human skin, soften blemishes, optimize studio lighting, and eliminate undesirable color casts.

**Total profiles in this category:** `25`

## Overview & Key Highlights

* **Canon Studio & Snapshot Portrait (Official)**: Canon's official portrait styles tuned to prevent oversaturating reds and yellows under direct sunlight or studio strobes.
* **LightFace & cportrait (V1 & V2)**: Curves specially designed to lift facial luminance and eliminate greenish/yellowish color casts.
* **Portrait Bounced Flash**: Tone curve adjusted for ambient light loss and bounced flash color temperatures.
* **BeautyShot & Bridal (Hanayome)**: High-key fashion and bridal aesthetics prioritizing translucent, fair skin tones.
